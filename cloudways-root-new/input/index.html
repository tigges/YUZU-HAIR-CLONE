Project ideas copy (verbatim summary)

Priority 1 (conversion + usability)

  • Hero rebuild: one-line value proposition + primary Book Online CTA + secondary Prices & Services CTA, with patch policy compact.
  • Service cards: 6 to 8 clickable categories with from-pricing and typical duration.
  • Booking confidence: cancellation summary, timing guidance, and what to expect before/after consultation.
  • Sticky mobile booking action remains visible while scrolling.

Priority 2 (trust + authority)

  • Review module with source badges.
  • Team highlights with specialisms.
  • Results gallery linked to Instagram / mirrored media.
  • Why Yuzu section with 3 to 5 clear pillars.

Priority 3 (UX polish)

  • Navigation simplification.
  • Contact UX upgrades: click-to-call, click-to-email, map context.
  • Accessibility and performance pass.
  • Policy clarity without friction.

Concept directions for future iteration

  • Japanese Minimal Luxury
  • Results-First Studio
  • Community + Care Salon

Implementation notes

Section modularity: each homepage area is wrapped in .section-block to simplify hide/reorder iterations.

Instagram feed sources local archived media under /media/ for reliability and speed.

Pricing cues in services are aligned to the current public Yuzu price list PDF link.
